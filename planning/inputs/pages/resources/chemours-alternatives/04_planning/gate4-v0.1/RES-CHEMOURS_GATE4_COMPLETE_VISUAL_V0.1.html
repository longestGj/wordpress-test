Technical Evaluation Guide

How to Evaluate a Chemours Titanium Dioxide Alternative

If your current reference is a Chemours Ti-Pure grade, start with the exact grade, application and qualification requirements. A brand name alone is not enough to identify a suitable alternative.

Explore Titanium Dioxide Products

Why the Exact Ti-Pure Grade Matters

Chemours organizes its Ti-Pure portfolio across applications including coatings, plastics and laminates, with product and application guidance for each area. When evaluating another source, the exact grade and the role it plays in your formulation or process matter.

Record the full grade name you use, along with its application and end use. These details provide a more useful basis for reviewing another titanium dioxide grade than the brand name alone.

Prepare Your Evaluation Brief

Use these four inputs to organize your evaluation. Start with the information you have, then add details as you review relevant product pages.

  1. Current reference and use. Note the exact Ti-Pure grade, application, formulation or system type, and end use.
  2. Process and handling constraints. Describe the conditions that affect how the pigment is dispersed, processed or incorporated in your system.
  3. Performance and acceptance criteria. List the properties your application depends on, together with the test methods, target values and pass/fail criteria you use.
  4. Documents required for review. Identify the technical, safety, quality or origin documentation needed for your qualification process.

Compare Documented Facts, Then Qualify the Grade

1. Build a shortlist

Use the Product Hub to find IKHLAS grades listed for your application or production process. Treat those relationships as a shortlist for review, not as replacements for your current grade.

2. Compare product-specific information

Open the relevant product pages and compare documented properties with the criteria in your evaluation brief.

3. Validate in your own system

Run your normal formulation trials and acceptance tests before changing supply.
