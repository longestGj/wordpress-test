## Establish the R-706 Reference

Chemours describes Ti-Pure R-706 as a rutile titanium dioxide pigment made with its proprietary chloride process for interior and exterior coatings. Its product page lists gloss and semi-gloss architectural paints, wood coatings, vehicle refinish and OEM topcoats, and coil coatings among the applications.

The February 2020 Ti-Pure R-706 Product Information sheet identifies titanium dioxide content as 93 wt% minimum. It labels the remaining values in its properties table as typical unless otherwise specified. Keep that label with every value you use: a stated minimum and a typical reference value do different jobs in a qualification plan.

Record the exact source and version behind your current specification, together with any internal requirements or control results that govern your use.

## Build an R-706-Specific Evaluation Brief

Start with the information you already have. Use these points to organize the brief, then add missing details as your evaluation progresses.

1. **Current reference:** the R-706 document name, version and values used in your specification or control.
2. **Coating system:** waterborne, solvent-borne or another route; resin or binder; pigment loading; key additives; and relevant dispersion, application or curing conditions.
3. **End use:** substrate, interior or exterior exposure, finish and the performance requirements that matter in the finished coating.
4. **Acceptance plan:** control formulation, test methods, numeric limits or comparison rules, required documents and the people who will approve the result.

This brief gives a selected Grade a defined test context. It does not identify a product for you or predict the result.

## Plan Tests Around the R-706 Evaluation Dimensions

Choose the dimensions that matter in your own formulation rather than treating every item as a required test.

### Appearance and optical response

Consider color or undertone, gloss, tint strength and hiding. Define the instrument, film preparation, substrate, film thickness and R-706 control used for each comparison.

### Formulation and process response

Consider wet-in, dispersion, viscosity or rheology, and stability through the process conditions that matter to you. Keep pigment loading, binder, additives, dispersion energy and application conditions controlled.

### Durability and mechanical requirements

Select exposure and mechanical tests from the coating's actual end use. Record the substrate, preparation, cure, exposure cycle, measurement method and acceptance threshold.

### Handling, where relevant

If powder handling affects your operation, define the receiving, storage, transfer or bulk-flow observations your team will record.

The R-706 product page highlights particle-size control and undertone, and discusses gloss retention and durability. The February 2020 Product Information sheet discusses wet-in, dispersibility and hiding. Together, these points help identify areas to examine; they do not supply acceptance limits for another product.

## Understand the Scope of the TS-6706 Announcement

Chemours' 19 February 2025 announcement describes Ti-Pure TS-6706 as its TMP- and TME-free version of R-706. The announcement reports that Chemours evaluated TS-6706 in water-based and solvent-based architectural and industrial paint systems, with attention to viscosity stability, color, gloss, tint strength, hiding and mechanical strength. It separately reports dry-pigment bulk-flow testing for TS-6706 at laboratory and silo scale.

The stated TS-6706/R-706 relationship is limited to these two Chemours grades, and the reported evaluation applies to TS-6706 in Chemours' program. When assessing a separately selected supply, use your R-706 control formulation, relevant test conditions and acceptance criteria to create a separate qualification plan.

[Read the Chemours TS-6706 launch announcement](https://investors.chemours.com/news-releases/news-release-details/chemours-launches-ti-puretm-ts-6706-tmptme-free-version-flagship) — published 19 February 2025; reviewed 6 September 2026.
