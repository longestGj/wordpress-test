# UK Rutile Titanium Dioxide: Active AD0086 Investigation and Import Registration

As checked on **7 September 2026**, the UK Trade Remedies Authority lists AD0086 as an **active dumping investigation** into specified rutile titanium dioxide goods originating in China. HMRC has also been directed to register the specified imports from **27 March 2026**.

Registration records imports for a possible later decision. It does not by itself set or charge an anti-dumping duty rate. The checked current official sources did not publish an AD0086 provisional or definitive anti-dumping amount.

## Current status: investigation, registration and measures

| Question | Dated answer |
|---|---|
| Is AD0086 active? | Yes. The TRA case page displayed `Active` when checked on 7 September 2026. It identifies the proceeding as a dumping investigation initiated on 3 March 2026 and shows a last-updated date of 2 September 2026. |
| Are specified imports being registered? | Yes. Trade Remedies Notice 2026/14 directs HMRC to register the specified imports from 27 March 2026 and says registration continues until a stop notice. No titanium-dioxide stop notice was located in the official sources checked on 7 September. |
| Does registration mean an anti-dumping duty is already being charged? | No. The registration notice does not set a rate. Any later liability described in that notice depends on a definitive remedy being implemented and on the terms of that remedy. |
| Is there a current AD0086 provisional or definitive amount? | The checked official TRA and GOV.UK sources did not publish one. This is a dated source check, not a shipment-specific customs determination. |

AD0086 began after an application alleging dumping and injury. An active investigation means the TRA is examining those allegations; it is not a finding that dumping or injury has occurred.

## Written goods scope and registration codes

The investigation and registration notice describe goods that meet **all** of these written elements:

- rutile titanium oxides in pigments and preparations based on rutile titanium dioxide;
- containing **80% or more by weight of titanium dioxide**, calculated on the dry matter;
- having **all types of particle size**, classified under Chemical Abstracts Service Registry Numbers `12065-65-5` and `13463-67-7`;
- with a titanium dioxide crystal structure of the **rutile form**; and
- originating in the **People’s Republic of China**.

The notice places the goods under commodity headings `28230000` and `32061100` and lists these UK commodity codes:

`2823000010`, `2823000030`, `2823000080`, `2823000083`, `3206110010`, `3206110030`, `3206110080`, `3206110085`.

For registration, the notice identifies additional code `8A60` for the specified goods. It excludes titanium dioxide with an anatase or brookite crystal structure and identifies additional code `8A61` for those exclusions.

These codes and descriptions are checkpoints from the notice. They do not decide the classification, non-preferential origin or treatment of a particular shipment without its full facts.

## Registration is not a duty rate

The registration notice describes a possible later consequence only if a definitive remedy is implemented. In that event, the definitive measure would determine any amount, and registered goods could become liable from a date up to 90 days before implementation of any provisional remedy.

That wording does not establish that a definitive remedy will be imposed, that collection will be retrospective, or what any amount would be.

The TRA’s published case timetable is expressly indicative and not legally binding:

| Indicative step | Published window | Current reading |
|---|---|---|
| Provisional measure, if required | July/August 2026 | The checked official sources did not show an AD0086 provisional-measure notice. The conditional window is not proof of an outcome. |
| Statement of Essential Facts | September/October 2026 | Indicative only; check the TRA case for a published event. |
| Final recommendation | February/March 2027 | Indicative only; it is not a promise of timing or result. |

The investigation period is **1 January to 31 December 2025**. The injury period is **1 January 2022 to 31 December 2025**. These are the TRA’s evidence periods, not the start dates of a duty.

## Prepare the shipment inputs before checking treatment

Ask the importer, broker and product team to assemble:

- the exact product name, form, crystal structure and dry-matter titanium dioxide content;
- the proposed UK commodity code and the basis used for it;
- the non-preferential origin evidence and the producer/exporter details;
- the shipment, entry and import dates;
- the additional code being declared and why it applies;
- the destination and declaration route: Great Britain or Northern Ireland;
- for a Northern Ireland movement, the relevant at-risk/not-at-risk facts and authorization context; and
- the purchase, invoice, transport and customs-entry records needed to support the declaration.

Use these facts to check the current official tariff and case notices. Do not infer a result from a supplier location, trade name, route, commodity code or additional code in isolation.

TiO2 Malaysia supplies Malaysia-origin titanium dioxide, but that commercial product-origin statement does not by itself determine UK non-preferential origin, AD0086 scope, tariff preference, exemption, VAT or a zero-duty outcome for a shipment.

## Keep four checks separate

| Check | What it answers | Where to verify |
|---|---|---|
| AD0086 investigation and registration | Whether the written product/origin scope and current case action may be relevant | TRA public file and current Trade Remedies Notices |
| Ordinary customs duty | The ordinary tariff treatment for the declared commodity and origin | Current UK Trade Tariff |
| Import VAT | The VAT treatment based on the transaction and import facts | Current UK Trade Tariff and HMRC guidance |
| Great Britain or Northern Ireland route | Which tariff route and additional at-risk facts need to be checked | UK Global Tariff or Northern Ireland Online Tariff, with current HMRC guidance |

Trade-remedy measures, ordinary customs duty and import VAT are separate checks. A Northern Ireland movement can follow a different tariff path depending on the declaration and whether the goods are at risk of entering the EU. This page does not decide which path or rate applies to a transaction.
