## 8. Time-sensitive trade control

### 8.1 Safe Gate 1 direction

The page may eventually tell buyers to verify the current UK tariff classification and trade-remedy position using official sources. A short dated status sentence is optional, not mandatory.

### 8.2 Gate 2 decision required

Choose exactly one approach after a fresh official-source check:

1. One short, date-stamped factual summary plus a route to `RES-TRADE-UK`; or
2. A neutral “verify the current UK trade position” block with no case-status summary.

Recommendation: use option 2 unless `RES-TRADE-UK` is approved, freshly maintained and route-ready at the same release checkpoint.

Gate 2 V0.1 applies this as a state-dependent choice: the default page uses option 2, while the additional dated paragraph and internal Trade Update action are enabled only when the current-source, content-approval, freshness and route conditions for option 1 all pass. Buyer Clean never shows these as competing choices.

### 8.3 Claim-status directions

- `Avoid or circumvent anti-dumping duty` remains `DO_NOT_RENDER_WITH_REASON / EXPLICIT_PROJECT_PROHIBITION` because the project may not market circumvention.
- A claim such as `Malaysia-origin titanium dioxide is exempt from the current UK measure` is not automatically rejected, but is `EXTERNAL_CURRENT_SOURCE_REQUIRED`; it needs exact product scope, current official authority, date and user approval.
- The Gate 2-eligible neutral direction is: `Malaysia-origin titanium dioxide is a product-origin fact. UK customs classification, tariff and trade-remedy treatment depend on the transaction and should be checked against current official UK sources.`
- No publication of a rate, classification, scope outcome or investigation result without current official evidence and approval.
- No competitor or media source may serve as the authority for current trade status.

### 8.4 Freshness contract

- Check TRA Public File and current GOV.UK notice at Gate 2 approval.
- Check again before Gate 5 approval if trade text is visible.
