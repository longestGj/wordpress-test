# Titanium Dioxide Supplier for the Netherlands

IKHLAS TITANIUM (MALAYSIA) SDN. BHD. provides Malaysia-origin titanium dioxide for industrial procurement and technical evaluation in the Netherlands. Review products for coatings, printing inks, plastics and masterbatch applications, or send us your application, quantity and delivery requirements for a quotation.

## Start with Your Application and Evaluation Requirements

Use the information you already have about the application, process and end use. These details help you compare product information and prepare a useful enquiry; they do not need to be complete before you begin.

The [Vereniging van Verf- en Drukinktfabrikanten (VVVF)](https://www.vvvf.nl/brancheorganisatie) groups its member-company market segments into sections. These cover construction, printing inks, automotive refinish, marine, protective and fire-resistant coatings, and roads or road marking. Use these contexts to describe your project; they do not determine which TiO2 grade to use.

### Coatings

For a coating project, state the system and end use. Add the relevant sector context, whether the system is waterborne or solvent-borne, whether it is intended for indoor or outdoor use, the substrate and the properties you need to evaluate.

### Printing Inks

Include the printing process, substrate, ink system and finished application. Add the appearance, opacity, dispersion and processing requirements you will use to assess a grade.

### Plastics and Masterbatch

Include the resin, processing route and finished product. State whether you are evaluating TiO2 directly in a plastic product or as an input to your own masterbatch production, then add the appearance, dispersion and processing requirements that matter to the project.

## Documents for Product and Supplier Review

For a Grade you are evaluating, request the TDS, SDS, COA or origin and supplier-qualification documentation needed for review. Select one Product Grade and one or more document types on the request form.

A Certificate of Origin is available upon request.

If you do not yet know the Grade, you may start the document request, but you must select one Grade before submitting. You can also review candidate Grades in the [Product Hub](/products/) or use “Not sure / Need help” on the [quotation request](/request-a-quote/).

Each document request uses one selected Grade and one or more document types. You may name other Grades in Additional Requirements as supplementary context, but the form does not assign document types to those additional Grades. Submit a separate request for each Grade when you need to specify its document types independently.

## Request a Quote for Delivery to the Netherlands

On the quotation form, select one Product / Grade or “Not sure / Need help.” If you are considering other candidate Grades, add them in Additional Requirements. Include:

- your application or target requirement, with the process and evaluation context you already know;
- the required or estimated quantity in metric tonnes (MT);
- the delivery location in the Netherlands, with a city or port if known;
- your company details and business contact information.

After you submit your quotation request, our team will review your requirements and contact you using the information provided.
