# Titanium Dioxide Supplier for India

IKHLAS TITANIUM (MALAYSIA) SDN. BHD. provides Malaysia-origin industrial titanium dioxide for buyers in India evaluating supply for coatings, plastics and masterbatch production. Review product information or send us your application, quantity and delivery requirements for a quotation.

## Confirm the Material You Are Buying

This page covers industrial titanium dioxide pigment Grades. For masterbatch production, TiO2 is an input to your formulation; a finished white masterbatch combines pigment with a polymer carrier and is a different purchase.

If physical form, purity or another product specification matters to your project, include it in the quotation request.

## Review Products for the Intended Application

Use the application, process and end-use information you already have. You do not need an exact Grade or a complete specification before reviewing products or requesting a quotation.

### Coatings

State the coating system and end use, whether the system is waterborne or solvent-borne, whether it is intended for indoor or outdoor use, the substrate and the properties you need to evaluate.

### Plastics

State the resin, processing route and finished product. Add the appearance, dispersion and processing requirements you will use to assess a Grade.

### Masterbatch Production

State the carrier resin, processing route and downstream product. Evaluate TiO2 as a pigment input to your formulation rather than as a finished white masterbatch.

Review the Grade listings in the Product Hub, then open candidate product pages for Grade-specific information. Compare that information against your requirements; the listings do not establish a recommended Grade for India.

## Product, Origin and Import Information

For a Grade you are evaluating, request the TDS, SDS, COA or origin and supplier-qualification documentation needed for review. Each document request uses one selected Grade and may include more than one document type.

A Certificate of Origin is available upon request.

If you do not yet know the Grade, you may start the document request, but you must select one Grade before submitting. You can also review candidate Grades in the [Product Hub](/products/) or use “Not sure / Need help” on the [quotation request](/request-a-quote/).

You may name other Grades in Additional Requirements as supplementary context, but the form does not assign document types to those additional Grades. Submit a separate request for each Grade when you need to specify its document types independently.

## Prepare a Clear Quotation Request

To compare quotations on a consistent basis, describe the material, quantity and delivery destination clearly. On the quotation form, select one Product / Grade or “Not sure / Need help.” If you are considering other candidate Grades, add them in Additional Requirements. Include:

- the application or target requirement, including whether the TiO2 pigment will be used directly or as an input to masterbatch production;
- any relevant physical-form, purity, process or evaluation specifications you already know;
- the required or estimated quantity in metric tonnes (MT);
- the delivery location in India, with a city, port or receiving point if known;
- your company details and business contact information.

After you submit your quotation request, our team will review your requirements and contact you using the information provided.
