# Titanium Dioxide Supplier for Belgium

IKHLAS TITANIUM (MALAYSIA) SDN. BHD. provides Malaysia-origin industrial titanium dioxide for procurement and technical evaluation in Belgium. Review products for coatings and plastics, or send us your application, quantity and delivery requirements for a quotation.

## Confirm the Product, Origin and Destination

The Product Hub lists Malaysia-origin industrial titanium dioxide pigment Grades for formulation and production evaluation. If you are sourcing for delivery to Belgium, use this page to review products and prepare your enquiry.

Liquid dispersions, laboratory reagents and finished coatings are separate purchasing categories. If your pigment requirement includes a specific physical form, state it in your enquiry.

## Describe What You Need to Evaluate

Use the application, process and end-use information you already have. You do not need an exact Grade or a complete specification before reviewing products or requesting a quotation.

### Coatings

State the coating system and end use, whether the system is waterborne or solvent-borne, whether it is intended for indoor or outdoor use, the substrate and the properties you need to evaluate.

### Plastics and Masterbatch

State the resin, processing route and finished product. Explain whether you are evaluating TiO2 directly in a plastic product or as an input to masterbatch production, then add the processing and appearance requirements that matter to the project.

### Import and Distribution

If you are sourcing for distribution or more than one customer project, include the application groups, reference specifications, candidate Grades and estimated quantity for the intended delivery in Belgium. Use non-confidential technical criteria rather than customer formulations.

Review the Grade listings in the Product Hub, then open candidate product pages for Grade-specific information. Compare that information against your requirements; the listings do not establish a recommended Grade for Belgium.

## Request Documents for a Selected Grade

For a Grade you are evaluating, request the TDS, SDS, COA or origin and supplier-qualification documentation needed for review. Each document request uses one selected Grade and may include more than one document type.

A Certificate of Origin is available upon request.

If you do not yet know the Grade, you may start the document request, but you must select one Grade before submitting. You can also review candidate Grades in the [Product Hub](/products/) or use “Not sure / Need help” on the [quotation request](/request-a-quote/).

You may name other Grades in Additional Requirements as supplementary context, but the form does not assign document types to those additional Grades. Submit a separate request for each Grade when you need to specify its document types independently.

## Request a Quote for Delivery to Belgium

On the quotation form, select one Product / Grade or “Not sure / Need help.” If you are considering other candidate Grades, add them in Additional Requirements. Include:

- the application or target requirement, including any product-form, process and evaluation details you already know;
- the required or estimated quantity in metric tonnes (MT);
- the delivery location in Belgium, with a city, port or receiving point if known;
- your company details and business contact information.

After you submit your quotation request, our team will review your requirements and contact you using the information provided.
