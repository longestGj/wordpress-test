# Brand and Entity Naming Policy

| Context | Approved identity |
| --- | --- |
| Website and commercial brand | **TiO2Products** |
| Domain | `tio2products.com` |
| Operating company and legal entity | **IKHLAS TITANIUM (MALAYSIA) SDN. BHD.** |
| Manufacturer/supplier company | **IKHLAS TITANIUM (MALAYSIA) SDN. BHD.** |
| Geographic origin and location | **Malaysia**; retain factual Taiping, Perak and Port Klang references |
| Legacy website brand | **TiO2 Malaysia** — deprecated |
| Chemical notation | **TiO₂** or **titanium dioxide**, according to context |
| Brand spelling | **TiO2Products** (no space, subscript, or alternate capitalization) |

TiO2Products is the commercial website operated by IKHLAS TITANIUM (MALAYSIA) SDN. BHD. The company is the manufacturer and legal actor; TiO2Products is the website and commercial brand. Do not use “TiO2 Malaysia” as the website brand in new runtime content. Do not describe TiO2Products as the legal manufacturer.

The `/about/` page is the authority for this relationship. Header, footer, visible logos, alt/aria names, WebSite Schema, SEO brand suffixes and buyer-facing references use TiO2Products. Organization Schema uses the exact legal company name. Product/application copy may use “listed Grade” or “candidate Grade” where the website name adds no meaning. Malaysia remains in legitimate country-of-origin, location, manufacturing and sourcing statements.

Historical source documents, revision records, exact-old migration preconditions and test fixtures may retain the deprecated name to explain or verify prior states. They do not define current runtime identity. Current seeds and rendered pages must follow this policy.

## Visual identity

The primary identity uses the approved geometric, faceted hexagonal symbol and the **TiO2Products** wordmark. The outlined wordmark places **TiO2** in Deep Navy `#0B2D5B` and **Products** in Teal `#0EA5A0`; white and Light Gray `#F4F7FA` support the symbol and reverse uses. The [approved design board](inputs/brand/logo/approved-2026-09-24/design-board.png) is a raster reference, so production vector paths are a reconstruction of that approved appearance rather than the original source vectors. `scripts/generate-logo-assets.py` rebuilds the assets from that reference.

Malaysia does not appear in the primary logo. It remains the company's factual origin, manufacturing location and sourcing context. The old particle/orbit/Malaysia-map symbol and TiO2 Malaysia wordmark are deprecated and must not be reused. The design board's marketing slogans are not approved website copy; the optional industrial titanium dioxide tagline is not part of the Header or Footer logo assets.
