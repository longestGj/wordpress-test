## Application evaluation refinement (2026-09-23)

Intent: `/applications/` answers what matters in each industrial use case. `/products/` remains the grade-selection center. The H1, existing module order and visual system remain in place. SEO Title is **Titanium Dioxide Applications | Coatings, Plastics & More**. The Hero now directs buyers to compare application requirements before opening candidate product pages.

Each of the five detail-page cards presents its application, a short system-specific description, Key evaluation factors, a detailed-guidance link, then the unchanged Grades to Review links. All factors below are review criteria, not claims that every listed grade achieves a result:

| Card | New evaluation factors | Detail-page approved source |
|---|---|---|
| Coatings | Binder/vehicle and loading; dispersion route and process history; substrate/film/cure; hiding/colour/gloss as separate endpoints; exposure/rheology/methods | `data/process-applications/coatings.json`: “Define the coating before comparing the Grade”, “Read optical results in the prepared film”, “Compare every endpoint on a declared basis” |
| Plastics | Resin and incorporation route; conversion and specimen geometry; whiteness/opacity/appearance; dispersion and defects; exposure and methods | `data/process-applications/plastics.json`: “Define your plastics system”, “Judge optical results in the finished system”, “Keep dispersion screens and defect observations separate”, “Build durability evidence around the use condition” |
| Masterbatch | Carrier and receiving resin; concentrate and article loading; concentrate processing; let-down/conversion; final-article endpoints | `data/process-applications/masterbatch.json`: “Define the two-stage system”, “Read each observation for the question it answers”, “Keep processing evidence within its stage”. The offered product remains TiO₂ input, not finished masterbatch. |
| Printing Inks | Ink vehicle/process; substrate/layer/cure; opacity/coverage/colour/pinholes separately; dispersion/rheology/storage; methods | `data/process-applications/printing-inks.json`: “Define the ink and print system”, “Judge opacity in the printed white layer”, “Read dispersion, rheology and storage together” |
| Paper | Furnish and addition; brightness; whiteness/colour; paper opacity; dispersion and retention observations/methods | `data/process-applications/paper.json`: “Define the paper result and system”, “Keep brightness, whiteness, colour and opacity distinct”, “Separate pigment information from paper performance” |

Specialty Materials is explicitly a CR-901 grade-specific review, with typical composition, listed specific-surface-area options, impurities and intended material/process as review points. Its primary CTA links to `/products/cr-901/`. No specialty application route or equivalence/qualification claim was created; source is `data/products/cr-901.json`.
